Linux force-upload

The panel cannot express it. The job queue is built strictly from Policy.PlatformsToPackage(), and CONTEXT.md's "a creator may do LESS than policy, never more" is asserted by a test. This design gives visibility and routes the real capability to a policy that names Linux for that project. A genuine force-upload needs the glossary changed first.

Does the out-of-sync warning ship?

It is not computable today. The only comparison available compares a cook time to an upload time and is wrong in four ways — and "publish without repackaging" makes the worst one common. Recommendation: ship the ledger without it until per-version upload markers land.

May "Entry point" leave the UI?

CONTEXT.md currently mandates the label. The row becomes "Level to load" / "Blueprint to load"; the term stays in code and the glossary. The amendment must land in the same commit or the next agent reverts it citing the glossary.

Delete or hide bUseExistingPakFile?

This design deletes it — a persistent project-wide flag that silently publishes stale content is indefensible once a one-shot menu verb exists. Speak now if a workflow leaves it on across sessions.

The word "Upload"

Requirement G asked for it, but CONTEXT.md lists upload as an _Avoid_ for Publish — upload is one step of a publish, not the whole. The header names a section, not the act, so it reads as compatible. Confirm, or amend the note.
